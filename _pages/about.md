## Selected Projects

### Calibration of Power-Type Derivatives for Rough Volatility with Jumps

Developed a neural-network-assisted calibration method for power-type volatility derivatives under rough volatility with jumps.<br>
Research guidance from Weixuan Xia.<br>
[Code](https://github.com/TenghanZhong/GPU-NN-Option-Calibration)

### Hybrid Heston–RNN Residual Learning for ETF Option Pricing

Developed a structural-residual pricing framework combining Heston pricing with GRU/LSTM residual correction, supported by statistical dependence tests and rolling out-of-sample evaluation.<br>
[Code](https://github.com/TenghanZhong/GRU-Heston)

### Hybrid Approaches to Crypto Volatility Prediction Using GARCH and GRU Models

Developed two hybrid GRU–GARCH architectures and evaluated multi-horizon cryptocurrency volatility forecasts on over 830,000 five-minute Coinbase observations.<br>
ECON 515: Time Series Analysis course project. Instructor: Ratika Narag.<br>
[Code](https://github.com/TenghanZhong/Crypto_Volatility_prediction)
